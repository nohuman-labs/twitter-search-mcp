# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, practical contributor community. Be constructive, assume good intent, and discuss technical disagreements on their merits.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and the sharing of private credentials or personal information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through a maintainer channel explicitly published by the repository owner. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, limit participation, or take other proportionate action to protect the community.

This code of conduct applies to repository discussions, reviews, issues, and other project-managed spaces.
